Pre-launch checks

✓mass to the target: M·k ≤ launcher capacity
✓power: P_available ≥ 1.2·P_required
✓slots: instruments ≤ FCS slots
◇data: D_ratio < 0.5 forbids a full success only
✓deadline: cycles ≤ mission duration
✓PM: total ≤ 40·deadline
✓trajectory modules on the table
✓budget: price ≤ 1.35·advance
7 hard checks; D_ratio is soft. A failed hard check means the mission is “impossible”: the cards come back, the advance is not spent, but the step is already gone. P_success < 0.5 is the player's risk decision, not a ban. The advance arrives at the start of the mission; the bonus is 0.25·advance (half of it on a partial success).
